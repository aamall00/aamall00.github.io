BIRD RUNNER

Duck. Dodge. Survive.

A calm pixel-art endless runner for Android. Tap to jump, swipe to duck, and see how far the ostrich can go.

Get it on Google Play · Free · Offline · No sign-in

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to jump.
  • Swipe down anywhere to duck.

The world speeds up the longer you last. Jump the cacti. Vultures fly at two heights: duck under or jump over a low one, but a high one sails clear over your head, and jumping into it is the mistake.

What it doesn't do

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud save. Your best score lives on your phone.
  • No in-app purchases and no banner ads. Ads appear only after some game-overs, or when you choose to watch one to revive.
  • No tracking by us. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ad SDK needs to serve an ad.
